Due Diligence Research and Analysis System

A structured company due-diligence research workflow built using LangGraph and Google Gemini.

The system takes a broad company research objective, breaks it into focused subtasks, performs targeted web research for each area, verifies important findings, and consolidates the results into a final executive assessment.

What It Does

The workflow organizes company due diligence across four key areas:

Financial Health — revenue, profitability, financial position, funding, and financial concerns
News & Recent Developments — important events, controversies, positive developments, and overall sentiment
Competitive Position — market position, competitors, competitive advantages, and competitive threats
Business Risks — legal, regulatory, operational, reputational, strategic, and technology risks

The purpose is to turn a broad and time-consuming research problem into smaller, focused investigations that can be handled systematically.

Workflow
1. Research Planning

The workflow starts at the Research Planning node.

The user provides the company and the research objective. Gemini analyzes this objective and produces a structured research plan containing:

Company context
Focus areas
Specific subtasks for each research area
Initial risk hypothesis

The broad due-diligence objective is therefore converted into smaller, clearly defined research tasks.

2. Fan-Out to Focused Research Nodes

After planning, LangGraph fans out the shared state to four research nodes:

                    Research Planning
                           │
                           ▼
                     Research Plan
                           │
            ┌──────────────┼──────────────┐
            ▼              ▼              ▼              ▼
       Financial         News       Competitive        Risk
        Research       Research      Research         Research


Each node receives the relevant planning information and independently focuses on its assigned area.

This separation allows financial, news, competitive, and risk research to be performed independently rather than asking one process to handle the entire investigation at once.

3. Web Search and Evidence Collection

Each research node creates search queries relevant to its area and retrieves web results.

The current implementation uses a combination of:

Predefined search query patterns for each research area
Additional tasks generated by the planning stage

The retrieved results are normalized into a common format containing information such as:

Source title
URL
Search snippet

The results are then converted into a research context that is provided to Gemini.

4. Gemini-Based Structured Analysis

Gemini receives the relevant research context together with the assigned research objective.

The model is instructed to analyze the available evidence without inventing unsupported information.

Each research node uses a structured output schema to convert the analysis into consistent results.

For example:

Financial Research
        ↓
FinancialAnalysis

News Research
        ↓
NewsAnalysis

Competitive Research
        ↓
CompetitiveAnalysis

Risk Research
        ↓
RiskAssessment


These structured results are converted into standardized findings containing information such as:

Finding
Category
Severity
Confidence
Supporting sources
Verification status

The findings are then written back into the shared LangGraph state.

5. Findings Converge into Verification

Once the four research branches complete, their findings converge into the Fact Checking node.

The fact checker prioritizes more significant findings and performs additional independent searches to determine whether the evidence:

Supports the finding
Contradicts the finding
Is insufficient to verify the finding

It also records potential contradictions between different research areas.

6. Final Synthesis

The verified findings, original research findings, and detected contradictions are passed to the Final Synthesis node.

Gemini then consolidates the information into an executive assessment containing:

Overall verdict
Risk rating
Confidence level
Key strengths
Key risks
Recommendation
Recommended next steps
Fact-check summary
Detected contradictions

Current Search Approach

The current version uses a hybrid query-generation approach.

Each research area has predefined baseline queries designed around its purpose. The planner-generated subtasks are then incorporated into additional searches to make the research more focused on the user's objective.

For example:

User Objective
      ↓
Research Planner
      ↓
Financial Subtask
      ↓
Baseline Financial Queries
      +
Planner-Specific Query Context
      ↓
Web Search


This provides a reliable baseline while still allowing the planning stage to influence the research.

Potential Improvement: Dynamic Query Generation

A future version can make query generation fully dynamic.

Instead of maintaining predefined queries for each research node, the specialist could receive the planner's subtasks and previous state, then use Gemini to generate the most relevant search queries for that specific investigation.

The flow would become:

Research Objective
        ↓
     Planner
        ↓
   Focused Subtasks
        ↓
 Specialist Node
        ↓
 Gemini generates
 research queries
        ↓
    Web Search
        ↓
 Search Results
        ↓
 Gemini Analysis
        ↓
 Structured Findings


This would make the research queries more adaptive to the company, user objective, and planning context while retaining the same overall workflow architecture.

Key Characteristics
Breaks a broad due-diligence problem into focused research subtasks
Uses LangGraph nodes to organize each stage of the workflow
Fans out the research plan into independent specialist research areas
Uses web search to retrieve supporting evidence
Uses Gemini to analyze retrieved information
Uses structured schemas to keep research outputs consistent
Collects findings through shared workflow state
Independently verifies important findings
Detects contradictory or insufficient information
Consolidates all results into an executive assessment
Supports Quick, Standard, and Deep research depths

Summary

The workflow follows:

Define → Plan → Break Down → Fan Out → Research → Analyze → Verify → Consolidate

The key idea is to avoid treating due diligence as one large research task. Instead, the objective is decomposed into focused areas, each area is researched using relevant evidence, the important findings are independently checked, and the results are finally brought together into one structured assessment.
